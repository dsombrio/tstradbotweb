Reps who know your territory — and your dealers.

You can hire reps who work a state, or you can hire reps who already know every lumber yard, door shop, and multi-family buyer in it. We're the second kind.

Why manufacturers choose us

The rep business is full of agencies that sign a contract and disappear. We built Tradition Sales on the opposite idea: that the best reps are the ones the dealers already trust when they pick up the phone.

When we take on a line, we treat it like our own. That means in-person calls, product training for our reps so they can talk to your customers with authority, joint ride-alongs with your leadership when you want to see the field firsthand, and quarterly reporting that actually tells you something.

What we're looking for

  • Building product manufacturers with a real product, not a one-off SKU
  • Brands ready for the South / Southwest (and willing to ship there)
  • Lines that fit non-conflicting with our current book
  • Margins that allow a dealer to actually make money stocking it

How we work

From first call to first PO.

01

Fit check

A 30-minute call. We talk about your product, your territory goals, and whether the fit is real. No pitch if it isn't.

02

03

Your team trains ours on the product. We ride along. We learn the spec sheets and the install. We're ready before the launch.

04

Launch & report

Calls, samples, training, follow-up. Quarterly reporting you can put in front of your board.
